The final project for MAE 3150 - Mechatronics was a competition where teams of three designed robots to compete against other teams. Given a robot chassis, an Arduino UNO, a few basic electronic components, and a $40 budget, we were tasked to design, fabricate, wire, and code using registers a robot. The robot's primary objective was to collect the most amount of cubes in an allotted time. 

![Competition Board]({{ "/assets/images/robot-comp/robot-comp-board.jpg" | relative_url }}){: .inline-image-r style="width: 200px; "}

<div style="clear: left"></div>

**My role:** <br>
In my team, my primary role was designing the physical part of the robot necessary to collect cubes. For cube collection, our team decided on a raised robot and simple outer perimeter design that would enclose the robot on three sides. We chose to laser cut all parts to keep costs low, so I designed the walls using Fusion 360 and laser cut them. I also designed a small platform for the breadboard to sit on that would allow all the electronics to sit stably in place that was composed of laser cut parts.

Along with our individual contributions, our team worked together to plan and test the mechanical, hardware, and software aspects of our robot, including testing and debugging code. 

<div style="clear: both"></div>

**My biggest takeaways:**<br>
For me, this project was a very valuable teamwork experience, particularly due to the small number of people on the team and the diverse range of skills necessary to make the robot. I learned how important is it to have a diverse skillset on such projects to maximize efficiency and productivity, but also that those who are not necessarily the most experienced in some areas can still provide very meaningful ideas and contributions. Finally, this project went very smoothly since our team's goals and expectations aligned, allowing us to put in what we considered a reasonable amount of time and effort and got results we were all happy with.  

This project was also a great opportunity to apply some of the concepts I learned in the Mechatronics course, particularly when debugging and brainstorming how to implement strategies.
